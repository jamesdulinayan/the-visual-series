Lagman Qualicare Family Clinic

This project centered on establishing a modern, highly accessible web platform for Lagman Qualicare Family Clinic to streamline patient discoverability and booking workflows.

The main development goal was to transition the clinic’s services into a reassuring, clean, and fully responsive digital interface. I structured the custom frontend using intuitive service categorizations, seamless booking pathways, and a mobile-first layout to ensure patients can schedule appointments and locate critical medical updates effortlessly.
